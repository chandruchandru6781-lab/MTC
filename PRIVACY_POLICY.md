# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 20, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the MTC Quiz Application ("we," "us," "our," or "the Application"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 User-Generated Content
We collect the quiz questions and answers you create and store within the application:
- Quiz question text
- Multiple choice options (A, B, C, D)
- Correct answer
- User responses to quiz questions
- Quiz scores and performance data

### 2.2 Technical Information
When using the Application, we may automatically collect:
- Your device type and browser information
- Application usage statistics
- Error logs and debugging information
- IP address (through Firebase Firestore access logs)
- Timestamps of your activities

### 2.3 Information You Provide
- Quiz questions and answers you create or import
- Search queries and filters you use
- Settings and preferences you configure
- Feedback or support requests (if submitted)

### 2.4 Information NOT Collected
- **We do NOT collect:** Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, etc.) unless you voluntarily provide it
- **We do NOT collect:** Payment information (Application is free)
- **We do NOT require:** User registration or authentication
- **We do NOT use:** Tracking cookies or analytics (currently)

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Legitimate Uses
We use the information you provide to:
- Display and manage your quiz questions
- Sync questions across all users accessing the Application
- Calculate and display quiz scores and performance metrics
- Provide application functionality as described
- Improve and optimize the Application
- Debug errors and technical issues
- Comply with legal obligations

### 3.2 Data Sharing
**We do NOT sell or share your data.** Your quiz questions and responses are:
- ✓ Shared only with other users of the Application (intentionally)
- ✓ Stored securely in Firebase Firestore
- ✗ NOT shared with third parties
- ✗ NOT used for marketing or advertising
- ✗ NOT sold to data brokers

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Where Data is Stored
- **Questions:** Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud)
- **Cache:** Browser localStorage (your device only)
- **Backups:** Google Cloud automatic backups

### 4.2 Security Measures
We protect your information using:
- ✓ Industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption (HTTPS)
- ✓ Encrypted data transmission and storage
- ✓ Firestore Security Rules restricting access
- ✓ No plaintext storage of sensitive data
- ✓ Regular security audits and updates

### 4.3 Data Integrity
Your data is protected through:
- ✓ Real-time synchronization
- ✓ Automatic backups by Google Firebase
- ✓ Version control for question changes
- ✓ Conflict resolution for concurrent edits

## 5. Your Rights

### 5.1 Access and Control
You have the right to:
- ✓ Access all your quiz questions and answers
- ✓ Edit or modify your data at any time
- ✓ Delete questions you have created
- ✓ Download your quiz data
- ✓ Clear your browser's local storage

### 5.2 Data Deletion
To delete your data:
1. Delete questions individually through the Application
2. Clear your browser's local storage
3. For Firebase data: Contact us for bulk deletion (see contact info below)

### 5.3 Data Portability
You can:
- ✓ Export questions via the Application's export feature
- ✓ Import questions in JSON format
- ✓ Share questions with other users

### 5.4 Withdrawal of Consent
At any time, you can:
- ✓ Stop using the Application
- ✓ Delete your personal quiz questions
- ✓ Clear your browser data
- ✓ Disable Firebase integration (use localStorage instead)
